Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CRM Messaging App ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with our application.

1. Information We Collect

The App may process the following information:

  • WhatsApp messages sent and received through the App.
  • Facebook Messenger messages sent and received through the App.
  • Attachments, including images, audio recordings, and documents.
  • Basic message metadata, such as timestamps and conversation identifiers.
  • Information necessary to connect and operate supported messaging services.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • Display and manage conversations inside the CRM.
  • Send and receive messages through WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.
  • Store and display message attachments.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

Messages and attachments may be stored on the application's server to provide conversation history and messaging functionality. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with third-party services, including Meta's Facebook Messenger Platform and WhatsApp-related services. Information transmitted through these services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by integrated messaging providers as necessary to deliver the requested messaging functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Messages and attachments may remain stored in the CRM while needed for its functionality. Users who wish to request deletion of their stored information can contact the application administrator.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
